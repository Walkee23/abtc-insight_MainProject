clinical_notes Rabies Prevention (WHO)

What is PEP?

Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP) is the immediate treatment of a bite victim after rabies exposure. This prevents virus entry into the central nervous system.

Why is time critical?

Once symptoms appear, rabies is 99.9% fatal. Immediate PEP after exposure can effectively stop the infection.

Wound Washing

Immediately wash the wound with soap and water for at least 15 minutes. This is the first critical step in PEP.
